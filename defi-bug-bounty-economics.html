Rewards set against losses

The sum of all 154 maximum rewards equals 4.8% of the 2026 losses. 16 incidents each lost more than $15,000,000, the largest maximum on offer. Of the 280 incidents with an amount, 197 lost more than $150,000, the median maximum.

Programs also limit what they pay for. 140 of the 154 open programs carry the Immunefi default exclusion for "attacks requiring access to leaked keys/credentials". The other 14 have no default exclusion text in the API. Key Compromise is the largest loss class in the table above. The two feeds use different labels, so read this as a rough match and not as an exact one.

What these numbers do not show

A maximum reward is an offer. It is not a payment. For the 154 open programs the API holds no payment data, so this page gives no payout distribution for them. The contest leaderboards cover ended contests only. They show the researchers that Immunefi lists, and this page cannot tell how many others took part.

The counts depend on the filter. Immunefi's own program list showed 169 programs on 2026-10-10. The filter on this page gives 154. Programs on other platforms are not counted.

The loss amounts are as DeFiLlama records them. They are not reduced by returned funds. The feed records $5,675,416 of returned funds across 8 incidents in 2026. Both feeds change, so read each number as of 2026-10-10.

How these numbers were computed

Program data comes from https://immunefi.com/public-api/bounties.json, fetched 2026-10-10 06:19 UTC. A program counts as open when isPaused is false and endDate is empty or later than the fetch time. The maximum reward is the maxBounty field, in US dollars. The scope count looks for the text "leaked keys/credentials" in defaultOutOfScopeGeneral. Contest earnings come from boostedLeaderboard. The earning of a row is totalEarnings when the row has it and earnings when it does not. The pool is rewardsPool.

Loss data comes from https://api.llama.fi/hacks, fetched at the same time. An incident counts for 2026 when its date is on or after 2026-01-01 00:00 UTC. The loss is the amount field, in US dollars, and an empty amount counts as zero. Groups use the targetType and classification fields. A group under 1.0% of the total goes into the "other" row. Shares are rounded to one decimal, so a column can differ from 100.0% by 0.1.
